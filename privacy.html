Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

rclone Drive Access is a personal application used by a single individual to access that individual's own Google Drive from their own computer through the open-source rclone tool.

Data access

The app requests access to Google Drive files only so that the account owner can read, write and organize their own files. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Data collection and sharing

This app does not collect, store, sell, or share any user data on any server. No data is transferred to third parties. All file transfers happen directly between the owner's computer and Google.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings page under third-party access.

Contact

For questions, contact the app owner through the email address listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
